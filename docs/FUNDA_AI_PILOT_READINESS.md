# Funda AI: controlled-pilot readiness checklist

Last reviewed: 2026-10-09, pre-merge audit on branch `claude/funda360-audit-0foiq8` (PR #9).

**Overall: NOT READY.** Funda AI must stay disabled in production until every gate below is VERIFIED COMPLETE. Passing CI does not open any gate on its own.

Status values:

- **VERIFIED COMPLETE**: done, with evidence.
- **CODE COMPLETE — NOT PRODUCTION VERIFIED**: built and tested locally; not yet confirmed in the deployment environment.
- **BLOCKED — HUMAN DECISION**: needs a legal, policy or business decision.
- **BLOCKED — CREDENTIALS OR ACCESS**: needs a secret, a dashboard or production access that the sandbox does not have or must not use.
- **NOT STARTED**.

## Order of work

1. Gates 1–4 and 15: decisions, recorded in writing.
2. Gates 5–7: production settings, made by the owner in the dashboard.
3. Gate 8: human review, then a merge. CI then applies the migrations and deploys the functions.
4. Gates 9–11: read-only checks after the merge.
5. Gate 12: set the key. Then gate 13: run the synthetic evaluation, then the human review.
6. Gate 14: the owner confirms who watches the usage queries, and how often.
7. Only then, a platform administrator in an `aal2` session enables `copilot` for one pilot school with an explicit role list and budget.
