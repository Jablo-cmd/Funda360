## Pilot success criteria
Measure login/activation success, data correctness, successful completion of agreed workflows, tenant/role isolation, report/statement correctness, usability, support demand and defect severity.

Use numeric thresholds only when they are explicitly agreed with the pilot school. Historical targets are not evidence of achieved results.

A successful pilot should end with documented acceptance, known limitations and a prioritised improvement backlog.
