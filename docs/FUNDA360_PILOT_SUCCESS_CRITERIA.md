# Funda360 — Pilot Success Criteria

Measurable criteria for judging whether the Funda360 pilot succeeded. Review these with the pilot school before starting, and again at the end.

## Technical

- [ ] Every provisioned user (principal, teacher, other staff) can log in successfully.
- [ ] No user ever sees another school's data — verified for the duration of the pilot (this was independently verified at the platform level before the pilot began; there should be zero exceptions during it).
- [ ] Teachers can record attendance for their assigned class(es), including correcting a mistake without creating a duplicate record.
- [ ] Principals can review school-wide attendance, including identifying learners below the 80% attendance threshold.
- [ ] Reports (Learners, Employees, Academic, Assessments, Attendance) can be viewed and exported to CSV without error.
- [ ] No critical security issue occurs (unauthorized access to another user's or school's data, exposed credentials, or a bypassed permission).
- [ ] No critical data-loss issue occurs (a saved attendance register, learner record, or other entry disappearing or being silently overwritten incorrectly).

## Usability

- [ ] The principal can complete their core workflows (checking learners/employees, reviewing attendance, running and exporting a report) without needing a developer to walk them through it, after reading the [Principal Quick Start](FUNDA360_PRINCIPAL_QUICK_START.md).
- [ ] A teacher can take and correct attendance without developer assistance, after reading the [Teacher Quick Start](FUNDA360_TEACHER_QUICK_START.md).
- [ ] Users can genuinely use the system on their own mobile phones — not just view it, but complete real tasks (e.g. a teacher taking attendance from their phone).

## Business

- [ ] The school reports that Funda360 provides practical value over their previous process.
- [ ] The attendance workflow measurably saves time compared to how the school recorded attendance before (paper register, spreadsheet, or another system).
- [ ] Principals report that the attendance report/reporting is genuinely useful for decisions they actually make (e.g. following up with a learner or family).
- [ ] Pilot users indicate, via the [Feedback Form](FUNDA360_PILOT_FEEDBACK_FORM.md), that they would want to continue using Funda360 after the pilot ends.

---

**How to use this document:** treat each unchecked box as an open question, not a failure — the pilot exists to find out the honest answer. Revisit this list at the pilot's midpoint and again at its close, using feedback forms and bug reports gathered along the way as evidence for each item.
