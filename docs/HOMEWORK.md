## Current implementation
Assignments support draft/published/closed lifecycle, due dates, instructions, rubrics/resources, learner submissions and resubmission, teacher marking/return/excuse, missing tracking, linked gradebook synchronisation, guardian/learner visibility and notification records.

A full LMS, video classroom, adaptive learning engine or AI tutor is not implied by this module.
