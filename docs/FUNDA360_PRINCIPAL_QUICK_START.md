# Funda360 — Principal Quick Start

A short, practical guide for your first session in Funda360. For full detail on any feature, see the [Pilot User Guide](FUNDA360_PILOT_USER_GUIDE.md).

## 1. First login

Go to the address given to you by your pilot coordinator, sign in with the email and password you were given. Leave **Remember me** unchecked on any shared computer.

## 2. Understand the dashboard

This is your home screen every time you log in. It shows real, live numbers for your school: active learners, active employees, number of classes, and today's attendance — plus, if you teach any classes yourself, a "My Classes" panel. Nothing here is a demo figure.

## 3. Check your learners

Sidebar → **People → Learners**. Confirm the learner list looks right for your school. Open a learner's profile to see their enrollment, guardians, emergency contacts, and medical information in one place.

## 4. Check your employees

Sidebar → **People → Employees**. Confirm your staff list is accurate. From an employee's profile, you (or your HR-provisioned staff) can provision that person a Funda360 login if they need one.

## 5. Review attendance

Sidebar → **Operations → Attendance** shows today's register for any class in your school. As principal, you can view and record attendance for *any* class, not just ones you personally teach.

## 6. Run an attendance report

Sidebar → **Reporting → Reports → Attendance**. Set a date range and, optionally, a class filter. You'll see:

- Present / Absent / Late totals and the school's attendance rate for that period.
- A "N learners have attendance below 80%" alert — click **View →** to see exactly who.
- Two breakdowns: **Attendance by class** and **Attendance by learner**.

## 7. Export a report

Click **Export CSV** above either table on the Attendance Report (or on any other report page) to download a spreadsheet of what's currently on screen.

## 8. Manage users, where permitted

Sidebar → **Administration → Users & Roles**. You can create new staff accounts here — the role picker currently offers **School Administrator**, **Principal**, and **Teacher**. Other staff roles (HR, admissions, etc.) are provisioned individually from that person's Employee profile instead, once they've been added as an employee.

## 9. Log out

Click your name (top right) → **Sign out**, especially on any shared device.

---

**That's the full first-session loop.** Everything above uses real data from the moment you log in — there is nothing further to "set up" before you start using it day to day.
