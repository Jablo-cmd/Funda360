# Grade 4 Mathematics Term 1 — Source Gap Analysis

## Purpose

This document compares the current Funda360 Grade 4 Mathematics Term 1 draft pack with the official DBE sources.

Sources:
1. CAPS Mathematics Grades 4–6, Intermediate Phase:
   https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/CD/National%20Curriculum%20Statements%20and%20Vocational/CAPS%20IP%20%20MATHEMATICS%20GR%204-6%20web.pdf
2. 2026 Grade 4 Mathematics ATP:
   https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Recovery%20plan%20page/2026%20ATPs/2026_ATP_Mathematics_Grade%204.pdf?ver=2026-01-20-202040-057

## Current draft coverage

### Covered
- whole-number counting/order/compare/place value
- four-digit number work
- addition/subtraction
- classroom problem solving
- estimation-related work
- number-line strategy
- formative assessment
- differentiated support/challenge
- low-resource delivery

### Missing or incomplete
- explicit odd/even numbers to at least 1 000
- explicit rounding to nearest 10, 100 and 1 000
- number sentences
- inspection/trial-and-improvement/substitution checking
- commutative property
- associative property
- distributive property
- additive property of zero
- multiplication
- multiplication calculation strategies
- financial-context problem solving
- measurement-context problem solving
- formal Term 1 assignment structure

### Incorrect term placement risk

The existing draft includes a Common Fractions topic in Term 1.

The 2026 ATP places Common Fractions later in the Grade 4 annual sequence. The Term 1 curriculum pack should therefore be reconciled before approval.

## Required remediation

1. Do not publish the existing Term 1 pack.
2. Keep the current pack as a historical draft until reconciled.
3. Build a corrected Term 1 curriculum structure from the authoritative ATP/CAPS sources.
4. Map every objective to an authoritative source section/reference.
5. Add lessons and toolkit resources for each required Term 1 concept.
6. Add low-resource alternatives for every lesson.
7. Add formal assessment coverage.
8. Have the resulting content reviewed by a qualified curriculum specialist.
9. Only then progress the version through review/approval/publication.
