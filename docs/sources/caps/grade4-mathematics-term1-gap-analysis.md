## Common Fractions: what was done and why

- The earlier draft has a topic `G4.MATH.T1.FRAC` with two objectives (`FRAC.01`, `FRAC.02`) in **Term 1**. It has no lessons, resources or questions of its own.
- The recorded 2026 ATP puts Common Fractions **later in the year**, not in Term 1 ([`grade4-mathematics-2026-atp.md`](grade4-mathematics-2026-atp.md)). The exact term and weeks are not recorded and were not guessed.
- Nothing was deleted. The Fractions topic and its objectives stay in the earlier version for history, and that version is marked as superseded (if still a draft).
- The reconciled pack contains **no** Common Fractions topic, objective or lesson. A test enforces this.
- Still to do: a later-term pack, once the term and sequence are read from the ATP.

4. **Weeks and hours.** The record gives none, so the term has no week count.
5. **Financial contexts.** Whether Term 1 expects cents or decimal amounts is not recorded. The pack uses whole rands only.
6. **Assessment.** The record says only "three hours in class" and the three areas covered.
7. **Document identity.** See [`curriculum-source-register.md`](../curriculum-source-register.md): the two CAPS URLs are not confirmed to be the same document.
