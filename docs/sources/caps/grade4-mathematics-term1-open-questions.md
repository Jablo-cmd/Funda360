## How these were handled

- Nothing in the pack was added "just in case" to cover a possible requirement, and nothing was removed. A guess in either direction would make the pack look more certain than it is.
- Every question is assigned to a curriculum specialist. Closing one means writing the answer, the document, edition and page it came from, and the reviewer's name in the verification checklist ([`../../verification/grade4-mathematics-term1-checklist.md`](../../verification/grade4-mathematics-term1-checklist.md)).
