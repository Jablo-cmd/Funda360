## Term 1 — authoritative scope

The 2026 Grade 4 Mathematics ATP identifies the following Term 1 content:

### Whole numbers
- Counting forwards and backwards in 2s, 3s, 5s, 10s, 25s, 50s and 100s between 0 and at least 10 000
- Ordering, comparing and representing numbers to at least four digits
- Odd and even numbers to at least 1 000
- Place value of digits in whole numbers to at least four digits
- Rounding to the nearest 10, 100 and 1 000

### Number sentences
- Write number sentences describing problem situations
- Solve and complete number sentences by inspection
- Trial and improvement
- Checking solutions by substitution
- Recognise and use commutative, associative and distributive properties of operations with whole numbers
- Understand 0 in terms of its additive property

### Whole-number addition and subtraction
- Addition and subtraction of whole numbers of at least four digits
- Calculation/checking strategies including:
  - estimation
  - building up and breaking down numbers
  - number lines
  - rounding and compensating
  - inverse operations
- Strategies must preserve conceptual understanding

### Whole-number multiplication
- Multiply one-digit numbers by numbers up to 10
- Multiply at least two-digit by one-digit numbers
- Multiply at least a two-digit whole number by a two-digit number
- Use strategies such as estimation, building up/breaking down, doubling/halving and inverse operations

### Problem solving
- Solve whole-number problems in:
  - financial contexts
  - measurement contexts

### Formal assessment
The 2026 ATP includes a Term 1 assignment covering:
- whole numbers: counting, ordering and representing
- number sentences
- whole numbers: addition and subtraction

The ATP notes that the assignment is completed in class within three hours.

## Important correction to the existing Funda360 draft

The existing Grade 4 Mathematics Term 1 vertical slice was created before official verification and included a simplified topic set containing:
- whole numbers
- addition/subtraction
- common fractions

The current 2026 ATP shows that this is **not a complete Term 1 representation**.

In particular, the draft must not be treated as a complete CAPS/ATP Term 1 curriculum because it currently lacks explicit coverage for:
- odd/even numbers
- rounding to 10, 100 and 1 000
- number sentences
- properties of whole-number operations
- multiplication
- financial-context problem solving
- measurement-context problem solving
- the formal Term 1 assignment structure

Common fractions appear in the 2026 ATP later in the year, not as a core Term 1 topic.
