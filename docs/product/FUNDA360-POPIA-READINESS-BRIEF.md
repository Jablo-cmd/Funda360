## POPIA technical readiness
Technical controls include tenant isolation, RLS/forced RLS, role-scoped access, guardian/learner relationship scoping, protected documents, consent records and auditability.

These controls do not constitute legal certification. Organisational readiness also requires lawful-processing governance, privacy notices, retention/deletion rules, data-subject request procedures, breach response, processor agreements and access reviews.
