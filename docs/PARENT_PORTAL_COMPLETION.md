## Current implementation
The guardian portal supports dashboard, children, profiles, academic results, report cards, attendance, homework, documents, timetable, behaviour, permitted medical/emergency information, admissions status, notifications, messaging and consent.

Relationship scoping and database/RLS controls govern visibility. Native mobile, richer payment workflows and broader automation remain expansion areas.
